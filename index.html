Frequently asked

Questions? Here are the basics.

Is the Google Trust Pack an official Google product?

No. LocalBoost AI is independent of Google. We prepare content for a business's Google Business Profile, and the business approves any publishing.

Are the gallery examples real client projects?

No. They are original fictional concepts illustrating the type and tone of work we can deliver. They are not testimonials or evidence of results.

Do I need to share my Google password?

No. We can deliver drafts for you to publish. If account access is needed later, use an authorized manager role rather than sending a password.

Do you guarantee more reviews, leads or sales?

No. We guarantee the agreed deliverables, not business outcomes, platform approvals or rankings. We never promise or create fake reviews.

Do I have to commit to a subscription?

No. The listed starter services are one-time projects. Recurring work is offered only after a separate agreement.

What if I need a correction or refund?

Each package includes one reasonable revision round within the agreed scope. We aim to resolve concerns and will refund amounts paid for uncompleted work if a project is canceled under the agreed terms. Completed work is non-refundable except where required by law.

How do I get started?

Choose a package, contact us with business details and follow the instructions on the onboarding page. Existing $99 orders can use the live Stripe link.
